## 4. THE "DRUG" MECHANISM (COMPLIANT PSYCHOLOGY)

### Variable-Ratio Schedule
- Fragments arrive on a **non-fixed schedule**
- Sometimes 2 weeks, sometimes 4 weeks
- Sometimes a physical artifact arrives unexpectedly
- Sometimes a voice recording instead of text
- **Unpredictability creates the strongest habit loops**

### Identity Reinforcement
- Each fragment refers to them by their **true name**, not their brand
- "You, [Name], have always known this."
- The story becomes about **their personal journey**, not just their business
- This is how religious conversion works — it's personal, not transactional

### Social Proof (Hidden)
- "Only 12 have received the full reconstruction."
- "The last relic was claimed by [famous brand]."
- "There are 3 fragments remaining before the next invitation."
- **Never reveal the full list. Always hint.**

### Loss Aversion
- "If you do not complete this fragment, the signal goes dormant."
- "The architecture will not wait indefinitely."
- Creates gentle pressure without being pushy
