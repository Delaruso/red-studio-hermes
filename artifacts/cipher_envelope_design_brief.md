## DESIGN PRINCIPLES

1. **RESTRAINT OVER DECORATION**
   - One material. One accent color. One action.
   - If it doesn't serve the signal, it is noise.

2. **WEIGHT IS COMMUNICATION**
   - The envelope must feel heavier than expected.
   - Paper weight: minimum 120gsm, ideally 200gsm cotton.
   - The hand remembers what the eye sees.

3. **TYPOGRAPHY AS HIERARCHY**
   - Only two typefaces maximum:
     - Display: a geometric slab serif or modern grotesk
     - Body: a refined sans-serif with generous spacing
   - Entity name in small caps, tracked wide, centered, bottom third only
   - No other text on the envelope exterior

4. **THE SEAL IS THE BRAND**
   - The wax seal is the only "logo"
   - It must be embossed, not printed
   - Red wax with gold flecks, not flat red
   - The seal placement is the only visual anchor

5. **HIDDEN DETAILS**
   - Inside flap: blind deboss of the sigil
   - Inner card: watermark of entity name + date
   - QR code: gold foil, not black print
   - Nothing visible from outside except the seal

## MATERIALS SPECIFICATION

### Envelope
- **Paper**: Black cotton paper, 200gsm, FSC-certified
- **Finish**: Matte, not glossy
- **Size**: A5+ (230mm × 165mm) — slightly oversized for drama
- **Color**: Deep black, not charcoal. Must absorb light.
- **Texture**: Minimal linen finish, visible only at angle

### Inner Card
- **Paper**: White cotton, 100lb, soft finish
- **Size**: 210mm × 148mm (A5)
- **Print**: Single color, dark gray ink, not black
- **Typography**: Set in small caps, wide tracking

### Wax Seal
- **Wax**: Deep red with gold flecks, not uniform color
- **Diameter**: 40mm
- **Stamp**: Brass, engraved sigil, hand-poured
- **Placement**: Back flap, dead center, 80mm from bottom edge

### QR Code
- **Size**: 25mm × 25mm
- **Print**: Gold foil stamp, not ink
- **Placement**: Inner card, bottom right, under sigil watermark

## TYPOGRAPHY SPECIFICATION

### Display Type (Entity Name on Envelope)
- **Font**: A geometric slab serif or modern grotesk
- **Size**: 9pt
- **Tracking**: +200
- **Case**: Small caps only
- **Color**: Dark gray (#222222), not white
- **Position**: Bottom center, 25mm from bottom edge

### Body Type (Inner Card)
- **Font**: Clean sans-serif with generous x-height
- **Size**: 10pt
- **Leading**: 14pt
- **Tracking**: +50
- **Color**: Dark gray (#333333)

### Sigil Text (Inner Card)
- **Font**: Same as display
- **Size**: 7pt
- **Tracking**: +300
- **Case**: All caps
- **Color**: Red (#ff0001), 30% opacity

## PRODUCTION NOTES

### Printing
- Offset lithography on cotton paper
- No digital printing — the dot pattern is visible and cheap-looking
- Registration tolerance: ±0.1mm
- Ink density: minimum 120% for dark gray text

### Wax Seal
- Hand-poured, not machine-applied
- Each seal slightly different — imperfection is the point
- Temperature: 85°C for proper viscosity
- Cooling: 15 seconds undisturbed

### Assembly
1. Inner card printed and dried 24 hours
2. QR code foil-stamped after drying
3. Envelope assembled, no glue visible
4. Inner card inserted
5. Envelope sealed with wax
6. Placed in rigid mailer, no additional packaging

### Quality Control
- Every 10th envelope inspected for wax seal alignment
- All QR codes tested for scannability
- Paper weight verified on digital scale
- No fingerprints on exterior — handled with cotton gloves

## WHAT MAKES THIS LUXURY

1. **Weight**: 200gsm paper feels like cardstock, not stationery
2. **Restraint**: No logos, no URLs, no branding
3. **Scent**: Cotton paper has a faint, clean smell. No perfumes.
4. **Sound**: The envelope opening makes a specific sound — not a rustle, but a sigh.
5. **Temperature**: The wax seal is warm when they receive it. The metal clasp on the mailer is cold.
6. **Time**: Hand-assembled means no two are identical. The slight variation proves a human was here.
