# RELIC PACKAGING TEMPLATE

## Materials
- Black rigid board, 2mm thickness
- Black linen paper, 120gsm wrap
- Gold foil stamping
- Magnetic closure
- Numbered certificate insert

## Template SVG
See: relic_box_sigil.svg
- Cut on 200x200mm board
- Fold lines at 50mm from edges
- Sigil centered on lid, gold foil, 40mm diameter

## Assembly
1. Cut board to template
2. Score fold lines
3. Wrap in black linen
4. Foil stamp sigil on lid
5. Install magnetic closure
6. Insert numbered certificate
7. Place relic in foam insert
