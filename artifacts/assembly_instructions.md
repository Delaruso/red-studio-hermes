# CIPHER ENVELOPE — ASSEMBLY INSTRUCTIONS

## Materials
- Black linen paper, 120gsm (A4 or larger sheets)
- Red wax seal sticks or sealing wax pellets
- Wax seal stamp (use sigil_wax_seal.svg as 3D print model)
- White cotton paper, 100lb (for inner card)
- Red cotton thread
- QR code sticker (2cm × 2cm)
- Glue stick or archival adhesive

## Cutting
1. Print `cipher_envelope_template.svg` at 100% on the black linen paper
2. Cut along the red dashed lines
3. Score along the gold dotted fold lines with a bone folder

## Folding
1. Fold the top flap down first (the triangle)
2. Fold left and right side flaps inward
3. Fold bottom flap up
4. The envelope should hold its shape without glue at this stage

## Inner Card
1. Cut white cotton paper to 140mm × 100mm
2. Print the cipher text on one side only
3. Fold once to 70mm × 100mm
4. Place inside envelope before sealing

## Sealing
1. Position the wax seal on the back flap center (as shown in template)
2. Melt red wax evenly
3. Press the sigil stamp firmly into the wax while warm
4. Hold for 10 seconds, then lift straight up
5. Let cool completely before handling

## Addressing
- No return address
- No branding
- Handwrite recipient name in white ink on the envelope front, bottom center
- Use capitals only: [ENTITY NAME]

## Shipping
- Place in rigid mailer
- No additional packaging
- No invoice, no business card, no return slip

## Cost Per Unit
- Materials: ~$4.50
- Labor: ~$8.00
- Postage (international): ~$15.00
- **Total: ~$27.50 per envelope**

## Notes
- Each envelope is numbered on the inner card in red ink: 01/12, 02/12, etc.
- The QR code links to a unique, password-protected PDF
- The password is the recipient's company name + "2026"
- If the PDF is accessed from an unexpected IP, it self-destructs and logs the attempt
