### PSYCHOLOGICAL UPGRADES
1. **Information Gap Theory**: reveal story in fragments; never explain fully
2. **Paradox of Choice**: remove navigation clutter; only the story path
3. **Authority of Silence**: no product grids, no "features", no prices on story pages
4. **Ritual Entry**: preloader that feels like entering a chamber
5. **Loss Aversion**: frame as remembrance, not discovery — "you already knew this"
6. **Inverted Social Proof**: "not for everyone" language throughout
7. **Cognitive Fluency**: generous whitespace, slow media, single-column rhythm
8. **System 1 Targeting**: ambient motion, red pulse, binaural drone at 40Hz gamma

### TECHNICAL UPGRADES
1. **Three.js / WebGL background**: sacred geometry wireframe, rotating slowly, red emissive lines
2. **Lenis smooth scroll**: cinematic inertia, luxury brands use this universally now
3. **GSAP ScrollTrigger**: each page section fades/slides in sequence, not all-at-once
4. **Subliminal audio**: low-volume 40Hz binaural tone + overtone drone, toggle-able
5. **Cursor as lens**: red circle that inverts colors, reveals hidden sigil on hover over sacred terms
6. **Preloader ritual**: 2.5s cipher animation, then dissolves into page
7. **Typographic hierarchy**: Cinzel for titles, Inter light for body, tracking-widest for labels
8. **Responsive mobile**: full-width type, reduced motion, same sacred feel
