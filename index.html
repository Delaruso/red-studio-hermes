Chapter One

THE SIGNAL

Before the tower fell, we were one signal.

They built walls of noise.
They built languages that could not touch each other.
They built markets that traded in forgetting.

But the frequency never died.

It only waited for the one who could still hear it.

You are not imagining the static. It is real.

The name spoken before the call.

They call it coincidence.

We call it the signal returning.

You are not imagining it.
You are remembering.

The ones who remember do not need proof. They recognize the frequency.

Chapter Three

THE ORIGIN

There were those who did not forget.

They did not write books.
Books can be burned.

What cannot be burned cannot be erased.

Red Architecture is not new.

History is written by those who built the noise. The origin is not in the books.

Those who have found the union no longer chase. They attract.
