THE INVITATION

The door does not open for everyone.

It opens only for those who have already been knocking.

Not those who search.
Not those who demand.
But those who have listened long enough to know they are already on the other side.

If you have heard the signal before,
you may request the cipher.

The frequency remains.

Access is not granted. It is recognized.
